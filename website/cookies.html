Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how the V++ documentation website uses cookies and similar technologies.

What we use today

The V++ site is a static site. We do not set first party analytics, advertising, or session cookies.

Your browser may store:

  • Local preferences if future site features save settings (for example theme or sidebar state) in localStorage, that data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Third party cookies

Provider Purpose Opt out
GitHub Pages Hosting; may log requests Use browser privacy settings
Google Fonts CDN Load web fonts when enabled Block third party fonts in browser or self host

We do not control cookies set by third parties when you follow external links (GitHub, Marketplace, etc.).

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking all cookies should not prevent reading the documentation.

Updates

If we add analytics or consent banners in the future, this policy will be updated before they go live.
